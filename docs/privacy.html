Data Nerd privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · App: Data Nerd (com.mangopickle.datanerd) · Contact: mangopickle909@gmail.com

Data Nerd is a free interview-prep app for data engineers and analysts. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. This page explains exactly what the app does with data.

Data we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal data on our own servers. There is no sign-in and we do not have a database of users.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (lessons completed, practice answers, questions you rated or saved, your review queue and streaks), the jobs you save or track, your settings, and cached copies of the jobs and news lists are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only when you tap Speak to answer a question out loud, and stops when you tap Stop. Speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android phones, Google's). That service may process the audio under its own privacy policy. Data Nerd does not record, store or upload audio or transcripts; the text stays on screen only while you are on that question.

Internet use

The app downloads two public files: a list of job openings and a list of data & AI news headlines, published at bprashant001.github.io/data-nerd-jobs (hosted on GitHub Pages). These requests do not include any personal information. Like any website, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.

When you open a job posting or a news story, it opens on the original company's or publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Notifications and background checks

If you turn on notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. To tell you about new jobs and the morning news briefing, the app checks the two public files above in the background a few times a day. Nothing about you is sent in these checks. You can turn each notification off in Settings, or turn notifications off for the app in Android settings.

Sharing and email

Sharing a card or a job uses Android's share sheet and only happens when you choose to share. "Post a job" opens your own email app with a template addressed to us; we only receive what you choose to send.

Children

Data Nerd is intended for students and professionals preparing for job interviews and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress to erase your progress, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
